# :book: Book List :book:
This book list covers all books and other such resources. Split into two broad sections, materials for all teams (and anyone that is new to this material who is most likely high school aged or older) and materials that are more advanced (or mostly more advanced) which are a better fit for college and industry. Resources listed in all teams have info beyond high school, but they have enough relevance for their cost to be useful for exposing high school students to concepts even if only one or two sections are suitable. 

*Note: Resources with a :mag: have an online option either the current edition or an older edition*

[OpenStax](https://openstax.org/) and [LibreTexts](https://libretexts.org/) are two good online freely available resources. Most of all of those are text book style and digital. You may find it relevant to swap in some of their python and java programming books for your needs.
