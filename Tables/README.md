# Tables 
A listing of various helpful tables and charts. I have found printing a few of the pdfs and package taping each page to a used UPS, FedEx, DHL, USPS, etc document mailer to be a great fit for a shop or tool box. Printing these off, putting in a plastic document sleeve, and hanging them somewhere can also be helpful. 

### Drilling
#### Drill Bit Listing ([link to chart](DrillBitList.md))
A list of drill bits Number 80 to 1 inch. 

#### Bob May Astronomy Drill Bit Master Chart ([internet archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20230321173000/https://bobmay.astronomy.net/misc/drillchart.htm))
This is a great simple master list of all drill bits up to 1 in.  A link to the internet archive is provided as this sit is no loner accessible. 

#### LittleMachineShop Tap & Clearance Drill Sizes ([LittleMachineShop](https://littlemachineshop.com/pages/tap-clearance-drill-sizes))
The printable chart covers both inch and metric.  

#### Imperial Supplies Drill Bit RPM Chart ([link to chart](https://www.imperialsupplies.com/pdf/3660-5_F_Drill_Bit_Sheet_Update.pdf))
Shows speeds for various metals and drill bit size combinations. 

### Fasteners
#### DB Roberts Fastener Reference Guide ([link to guide](https://www.dbroberts.com/cms/resources/literature/db-roberts-fastener-reference-guide/))
A good quick overview of the different styles of fasteners. Show various heads, nuts, washers, and thread types. Laminated versions of these are given out at trade shows. You may be able to have their marketing group send you a few or the used document mailer method can work.

#### Imperial Supplies Fastener Torque Chart ([link to chart](https://www.imperialsupplies.com/pdf/A_FastenerTorqueCharts.pdf))
Gives a good overview of min and max torques for different sizes and grades. 

### Electrical 
#### AWG Metric Comparison Chart ([link to chart](WireSizeCompareChart.md))
Compares AWG to metric cross section sizes. 

#### Molex Visual Inspection of Crimped Terminals ([link to chart](https://www.mouser.com/pdfDocs/MolexCrimpingUpdateEnglish.pdf))
Chart showing visual examples of acceptable and reject crimps for closed and open barrel terminals.
