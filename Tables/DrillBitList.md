## List of Drill Bits
Starts at number 80 and increases in size. Both inch and millimeter are shown to allow for fast comparison. The table contains Number, Letter, Fractional, and Metric bits to allow for selection of sizes close if you are lacking the exact size need. This can be helpful for prototypes if the context for your application allows this type of deviation. Significant figures are kept consistent for improved readability. Therefore, listed diameters do not imply that the bit or the method used to drill the hole will produce a hole of the listed size. 

### This chart covers drill bit sets as follows:
- Numbers 1 to 80
  - Note that 1 to 60 sets are more economical and more relevant for teams and prototyping. Rarely will situations come up where you will need to use drill bits smaller in diameter than number 35.
- Letter
- Fractional 1/32 in to 1/2 in with 1/64 in steps
  - Economical sets will cover this range typically starting with 1/16 in and may skip some sizes or vary the step size they use. Its important to check which sizes are included and if those work well with fastener sizes you will be using.  
- Fractional 1/2 in to 1 in with 1/32 in steps
  - It is typically more economical to buy bits in this size range as needed unless your are frequently using fasteners in this range.  
- Metric 1 mm to 6 mm with 0.1 mm steps
- Metric 6 mm to 13 mm with 0.5 mm steps
  - There are many sets that cover 1 mm to 13 mm with 0.5 mm steps and that can be more than sufficient in use cases where metric fasteners are not common.
